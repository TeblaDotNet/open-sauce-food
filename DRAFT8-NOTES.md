# Language design questions

This public summary replaces private historical stress-test and corpus-review notes.
It does not alter the Draft 8 grammar. Outstanding topics include process parameter
roles, conditional scope, output relationships, local part identity, compound-to-part
references and preservation of source precision during future conversions.

See [implementation questions](IMPLEMENTATION-ISSUES.md), [roadmap](ROADMAP.md)
and the authoritative [specification](SPEC.md). Proposals need concrete, distributable
recipe cases and explicit review; parser success alone does not settle language design.
