Crab salad
Source: https://publicdomainrecipes.com/crab-salad/
Source author: Not stated
Stated licence: Public domain (Unlicense) (site-wide)

Image: Crab salad (https://publicdomainrecipes.com/pix/crab-salad.webp)
⏲️ Prep time: 10 min
🍳 Cook time: 30 min
🍽️ Servings: 3

Ingredients
Сrab meat (300 grams)
Egg (3)
Rice (50 grams)
Corn (1 can)
Cucumber (1)
Carrot (1)
Onion (1)
Mayonnaise (100 grams)

Directions

1. Boil rice, eggs and carrot
2. Chop crab meat and all other ingredients
3. Combine and mix all ingredients
4. Add mayonnaise
5. Chill before serving
